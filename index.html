Eco-Friendly Custom Paper Bag Manufacturer

Established in 2020 in Malappuram, Kerala, Made Products is a trusted manufacturer and wholesale supplier of eco-friendly paper packaging solutions. We specialize in producing customized Kraft paper bags, textile shop bags, mobile shop bags, gents shop bags, jewellery bags, cake bags, and event paper bags for businesses across various industries.

Our manufacturing unit in Chelari, Malappuram delivers high-quality packaging solutions designed to meet modern branding and packaging requirements. With multiple customization options including logo printing, paper quality selections, custom dimensions, and handle styles, we help businesses create a strong and lasting brand presence. We are proud to serve more than 1000+ happy clients across Kerala, India, and international markets.
